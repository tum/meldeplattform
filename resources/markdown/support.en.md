You don't have to file a report right away. Many offices offer confidential,
non-binding advice – anonymously if you wish, and even if you are not sure yet
whether "it counts".

## In immediate danger

- **Police: 110** · **Emergency / ambulance: 112**

## Support at TUM

- **Diversity & Inclusion Office** – advice on discrimination, harassment and
  conflicts: [diversity@tum.de](mailto:diversity@tum.de)
- **Gender Equality Office** – advice on sexual harassment and sexualised
  violence: [gender@tum.de](mailto:gender@tum.de)

## Ways to report at TUM

- **TUM SafeSignal** (this platform) – in writing, anonymously if you wish,
  around the clock.
- **TUM Compliance Office** – reports about misconduct, harassment,
  discrimination and corruption can also be made in person, by phone or by
  email.
- **TUM IT Security** – IT security incidents also by email to
  [it-sicherheit@tum.de](mailto:it-sicherheit@tum.de).

## Support outside TUM

- **Violence Against Women Support Hotline: 116 016** – 24/7, free of charge,
  anonymous and in many languages.
- **Sexual Abuse Helpline: 0800 22 55 530** – free and anonymous (German).
- **Weisser Ring victim helpline: 116 006** – support for victims of crime.
- **Federal Anti-Discrimination Agency** – advice on discrimination:
  0800 546 546 5.

## External reporting channel

Instead of reporting to TUM, you can also contact the **Federal external
reporting office at the Federal Office of Justice** (§ 19 HinSchG).
