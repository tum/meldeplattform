## Who handles my report?

Each area on the start page has a fixed responsible office. Your report can
only be read by the people named there and a few technical administrators of
the platform – not your supervisor, not your lecturers and not the person you
are reporting.

- **Misconduct, harassment, violence, discrimination, academic misconduct,
  corruption:** the **TUM Compliance Office**. It reviews reports
  independently, i.e. free from instructions by university management.
- **IT security:** **TUM IT Security**.

## How does the platform protect my anonymity?

- **No account required.** You do not need to log in or give your name.
- **Access code instead of a name.** After submitting you receive a code. You
  use it to read replies and write back. The code is shown only once and
  stored only as an irreversible hash – the case handlers cannot see it
  either.
- **No IP addresses stored with your report.** The platform stores neither your
  IP address nor a browser fingerprint with your report.
- **Image metadata is removed.** For uploaded photos we automatically strip
  e.g. GPS location and device data. This is not possible for PDF, Office or
  audio files – please check them beforehand (e.g. the author name in the file
  properties).
- **Email only if you want.** If you enter an email address, we only use it to
  notify you of new messages. Without an email you stay fully anonymous; just
  check back via “Track a report” with your code.

**Tip:** If you want to stay anonymous, leave out details only you could know
(e.g. a one-on-one conversation), and avoid writing from a work computer in the
affected unit.

## What does the reported person learn?

Your identity is treated confidentially under the German Whistleblower
Protection Act (§ 8 HinSchG). It may only be disclosed in exceptional cases
defined by law, e.g. at the request of a law-enforcement authority
(§ 9 HinSchG). In that case you will be informed beforehand unless this would
jeopardise the investigation.

The reported person has the right to respond and to present a counter-statement
before any measures are taken. They learn *what* they are accused of – not
*who* made the report. Details that could point to you are left out wherever
possible.

## Retaliation is prohibited

Anyone who reports in good faith should not suffer any disadvantage for doing
so. For employees this is protected by law: retaliation is prohibited
(§ 36 HinSchG).

## Do I have to use SafeSignal?

No. SafeSignal is one channel among several. You can also contact a support or
reporting office in person, by phone or by email – or simply get advice first
without reporting anything. See [Support & other ways to report](/support).
